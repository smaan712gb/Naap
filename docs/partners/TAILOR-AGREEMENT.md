## The arrangement — معاہدہ

Naap sends you **stitching orders with a complete digital parchi** — the
customer's naap measured by the app, ease already applied, in your own
words (teera, chaati, kamar, ghera, paincha), with fit chosen and style
notes. You stitch, we handle the customer, shipping, and payment.

## Terms — شرائط

1. **Turnaround — مدت**: 7 working days from fabric-in-hand for standard
   garments; 12 for occasion wear. You confirm each order before the
   clock starts.
2. **Fit guarantee — فٹنگ کی ضمانت**: if a garment misses the parchi
   numbers beyond darzi tolerance (±½ inch on stitched dimensions), the
   remake is on the house — Naap supplies replacement fabric, you supply
   the stitching. If the parchi itself was wrong, remake cost is fully
   on Naap. Honest tape measurement decides, no arguments.
3. **Quality bar**: finished garment is measured against the parchi
   before dispatch (the app's tape-check screen makes this a 2-minute
   job) — that measurement is your proof of correct work.
4. **Payment — ادائیگی**: weekly settlement, JazzCash/Easypaisa/bank —
   آپ کی مرضی. Never held pending customer delivery.
5. **Exclusivity: none.** Your shop stays yours; Naap is extra work at
   your own rates. We only ask that Naap orders are not subcontracted
   without telling us.
6. Either side can end the arrangement with two weeks' notice; open
   orders finish at agreed rates.

## Why this is good business — فائدہ

- Orders arrive with exact numbers — no customer visits, no re-measuring,
  no "thora sa loose kar dein" cycles.
- Diaspora customers pay dollar prices; your rate reflects that.
- Every parchi carries "Measured at" — your shop's name travels on every
  order you stitch, worldwide.
