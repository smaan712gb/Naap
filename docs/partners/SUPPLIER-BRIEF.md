# Naap — fabric supplier brief

*Send this (or say it on the phone) to a Faisalabad/Lahore wholesale
contact. It states exactly what we need; nothing exotic.*

## Who we are

Naap (getnaap.com) is a measurement-first tailoring house selling
Pakistani cloth worldwide — unstitched cuts and made-to-measure stitching
against the customer's phone-measured naap. Catalog runs three tiers:
everyday (lawn, latha, wash & wear), occasion (boski, organza), and
ceremonial (raw silk, velvet, jamawar, zardozi work).

## What we buy (initial)

Small, repeatable quantities — we sell cuts, not bolts wholesale:

| Cloth | Cut | Initial monthly volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lawn / latha / wash & wear | 5 m suits | 10–30 cuts |
| Boski (6lb / 8lb / 12lb) | 5 m | 5–15 cuts |
| Raw silk, velvet | 4.5–5 m | 5–10 cuts |
| Jamawar / banarsi (occasion) | 4.5–5 m | on order |

## What we need from you

1. **A price list** per cloth per meter (with quality grade), and your
   trade terms (advance %, delivery days to a Lahore/Faisalabad address).
2. **Honest composition** per cloth ("boski" label vs actual fiber —
   we publish disclosed composition and never overclaim silk content).
3. **Provenance facts, only if true**: mill name, city, loom type. We
   print these on the product page with your name — free marketing for
   you — but only statements you stand behind.
4. **Swatches**: 10×10 cm cuttings of everything, monthly. We sell a
   swatch box to overseas customers; your cloth in it = your cloth
   ordered.
5. Photos are OUR job — we shoot everything ourselves; nothing needed.

## How payment works

We pay per order or weekly settlement, bank transfer / Raast / JazzCash —
your preference. No consignment ask, no returns of cut fabric.
