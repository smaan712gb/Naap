# Luxury outreach kit — who to contact, what to say, what never to say

## Positioning discipline (read first)

We do NOT pitch "we know your sizes" — a house hears that as IP theft.
We pitch the **clienteling problem they already have**: remote clients,
trunk-show follow-ups, returned MTM garments, fittings across time zones.
Naap carries the *measured body* to their own patterns; their blocks,
ease philosophy, and client book never touch our system. Brand names
never appear in our marketing (nominative size references inside the app
are legal; "as used by X" is not — never say it).

The privacy line is the door-opener with this clientele:
**no photograph ever leaves the client's device — architecturally.**

## Who to write (in order of likely yes)

1. **Independent EU/UK/US MTM tailors and small ateliers** (5–50
   clients/yr, no dev team). Fastest yes; they feel remote-fitting pain
   weekly and can pilot alone. Find: bespoke/MTM tailors with trunk-show
   pages, Savile Row outliers, Italian sartorie with US clients.
2. **MTM programs of premium retailers** (department-store MTM desks,
   suit specialists like MTM-focused chains). Innovation contact or MTM
   program manager.
3. **Luxury houses' client-development / retail-innovation teams** —
   longest cycle, biggest prize (white-label iPad clienteling). Reach
   via LinkedIn title search: "client development", "retail excellence",
   "clienteling", "omnichannel innovation" + the house name.

## The email (short version — adapt, don't lengthen)

> Subject: A fitting without the fitting — 2-minute demo
>

> them, fly to them, or guess. We built a third option: they stand in
> front of their phone for two minutes at home, and your atelier

>

>
> Live demo (no signup): getnaap.com/pro — the spec engine on that page
> is our production API.
>
> Proposal: a quiet five-client pilot over one trunk season. Your master
> judges the tape. If it doesn't hold to your standard, we part as
> friends.
>
> — Saad Maan, founder, Naap · getnaap.com

## What we can honestly claim today (and only this)

- On-device measurement, EN/Urdu voice-guided capture, photos deleted
  on-device (architecture, verifiable in the app).
- Tape validation: professional tailor's tape agreement on waist within
  0.1" on the calibrated build; validation program ongoing (say
  "early", never "certified").
- Dual-layer spec + DXF export live in production (the /pro demo).
- Posture profiling, drift reports, multi-client books for tailors.
- 151-entry chart-verified size library and growing.

Do NOT claim: brand partnerships (none), certification (none yet),
accuracy percentages we haven't measured across N bodies.

## Before the first luxury-house (not indie-tailor) call

- DPA/privacy review: B2B clienteling brings identity-adjacent data —
  blueprint flags revisiting the LLM-provider posture at that point.
- A signed pilot letter template (one page, mutual NDA optional).

## The ask ladder

Indie tailor: pilot now → testimonial → fit-library partnership
(they measure garments on tables anyway — pay per verified entry).
Retail MTM: pilot → per-client SaaS fee.
House: white-label capture + clienteling iPad view → annual license.
