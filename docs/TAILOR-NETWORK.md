# Tailor network — vetting, onboarding, and quality

The One-Click Stitch promise is only as good as the worst tailor in the
network. This is the working standard for admitting and keeping darzis.
Pilot: Imran Khan (Lahore-side contact, WhatsApp +92 300 1604333) is
candidate #1 and the reference case for this whole document.

## Vetting criteria (must pass all)

1. **Parchi literacy** — given a Naap parchi (three-fit table, highlighted
   column, style box), the tailor cuts from it *without a clarifying phone
   call*. Test: send a real parchi, ask them to read back what they would
   cut. This is the single most predictive test.
2. **Sample garment** — one shalwar kameez stitched from a Naap parchi in
   the fabric class they will serve (daily-wear lawn/latha at minimum).
   Judged on: measurement fidelity (±1.5 cm on girths vs the stitch
   column), seam quality, collar/bann execution, hem/paincha finish.
3. **Turnaround honesty** — quoted days vs actual days on the sample. A
   tailor who says 5 and delivers in 7 fails; one who says 10 and delivers
   in 9 passes. The network sells predictability, not speed.
4. **WhatsApp reliability** — responds within one business day, can send
   photo updates at cut/stitch/QC stages (photos of the GARMENT only —
   never of customers, per product law).
5. **Capacity declaration** — honest orders-per-week number. Start every
   tailor at max 2 concurrent Naap orders regardless of declared capacity.

## Onboarding steps

1. Vet per above (sample garment paid for by Naap — it's our cost of
   diligence, ~PKR 3–5k).
2. WhatsApp onboarding note (Urdu) covering: the parchi format, the
   highlighted-column rule, style box terms, photo-update expectations,
   and the no-customer-contact rule (all customer communication flows
   through Naap — the network must not disintermediate itself).
3. Registered in the order state machine (fabric_cut → stitching →
   quality_check transitions are theirs to trigger via admin for now;
   a tailor-facing status page comes when volume justifies it).
4. First three live orders reviewed garment-in-hand (diaspora orders:
   reviewed by the Lahore/Karachi QC contact before shipping).

## Ongoing quality

- **Strike system**: measurement miss beyond tolerance, blown deadline
  without proactive notice, or a customer-contact violation = strike.
  Two strikes in a quarter → paused from new orders; three → removed.
- **Scorecard per tailor** (kept simple, in the order history): on-time
  rate, first-pass QC rate, tolerance hit rate.
- **Payment terms**: 50% on fabric_cut confirmation, 50% on QC pass.
  Never full prepayment (removes urgency), never full postpayment
  (unfair to the tailor; kills trust).
