The idea — تعارف

Two photographs become a parchi your tailor can cut from.

Naap reads your measurements with your phone's camera, applies real tailoring ease — the asan a master darzi would add — and writes a bilingual parchi you send on WhatsApp. Your photos are analysed on the device and deleted. Only numbers leave. No account. No studio visit. No guessing.

The Naap promise — وعدہ

Your photographs never leave your phone. They are analysed on the device and deleted immediately. Your measurements are stored only on your own phone, and the parchi carries numbers — never imagery.

Beyond the parchi

Never buy the wrong size again.

One scan yields a Size Passport — your measured EU, IT, FR, UK and US sizes for shopping without the returns lottery. For bespoke clients, a Body Drift Report shows your tailor precisely how you have changed since the last fitting — no flight required. And in the Naap atelier, cloth is cut to your exact scan.
