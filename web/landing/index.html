How it works

یہ کیسے کام کرتا ہے
1

Stand in front of your phone

Prop it up, step back, and the app coaches you into place — with a voice, in Urdu or English — then takes the photos itself.

2

Measured on your phone

On-device AI reads your body measurements and applies traditional asan for the garment, fit, and fabric you choose.

3

Review & adjust

Every number is editable. Choose fitted, regular, or loose; pick your neckline, sleeve, and daman style.

4

WhatsApp it to your darzi

A clean bilingual PDF parchi with the exact terms tailors use — Teera, Chaati, Kamar, Ghera, Paincha.

🔒
The Naap promise: your photos never leave your phone. They are analyzed on the device and deleted immediately. No account is required, and your measurements are stored only on your own phone.
آپ کی تصاویر کبھی فون سے باہر نہیں جاتیں — تجزیہ فون پر ہوتا ہے اور تصاویر فوراً حذف کر دی جاتی ہیں۔

Never buy the wrong size again

اب سائز کبھی غلط نہیں

One two-minute scan gives you a Size Passport — your measured EU, IT, FR, UK and US sizes for online shopping without the returns lottery. Your tailor gets exact numbers; your favourite stores get the right size, first time. And for bespoke clients: a Body Drift Report shows your tailor exactly how your measurements changed since your last fitting — no flight required.
