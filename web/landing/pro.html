For made-to-measure houses & clienteling teams

Your blocks stay yours. We carry the body.

Naap is not a sizing opinion. It delivers the measured body — chest, waist, seat, shoulder, posture profile, drop — as structured data your own patterns and conventions consume. Nothing about your house's cut, ease philosophy, or client book ever enters our system.

Dual-layer spec

Layer A: raw biometric truth in centimetres. Layer B: a mapped garment block with per-field deltas from the nominal size — the format a cutter or CAD operator actually uses.

CAD-ready

Alteration deltas export as .DXF for Gerber/Lectra workflows. Posture profile (shoulder slope class, balance) rides along for the pattern room.
